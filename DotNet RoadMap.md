# DotNet Roadmap…

A structured learning path for anyone who wants to become a **.NET Backend Developer**, starting from C# fundamentals and progressing toward building real-world backend applications.

This roadmap is divided into three main stages: **Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced**, followed by additional topics for backend specialization.

The focus is on understanding concepts, practicing consistently, and building projects throughout the learning journey.

**How to Use**

Each week follows **Topics → Resources → Task**. Follow the weeks in order within each stage; complete the linked resource(s), then finish the task before moving on. Keep your exercises and deliverables in a (GitHub) repository so your progress becomes a portfolio.
